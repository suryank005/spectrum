24-HOUR FLAGSHIP HACKATHON

NATIONAL LEVEL HACKATHON

A 24-hour national computing battleground. Teams are free to choose any domain or field of interest to propose an innovative solution in the preliminary round, and compete on-site to build working prototypes in the grand finals.

DURATION
24 Hours Non-Stop
TEAM SIZE
4 Members
DATE & TIME
September 22 & 23, 09:30 AM
VENUE DECK
IT Block

NATIONAL HACKATHON STAGES & FORMAT

Stage 01
Preliminary Round

Teams must submit a PowerPoint presentation (PPT) presenting their proposed solution to a real-world problem. Teams are free to choose any domain, field, or area of interest and propose an innovative solution.

Stage 02
Final Round (Top 20)
  • The top 20 ideas from the preliminary round will be shortlisted for the finals.
  • Shortlisted teams must build and present a working solution at the event venue.
  • Finalists will be provided with an on-the-spot problem statement/theme during the event and will be required to design and build a working solution within the given time.

FLAGSHIP 24H HACKATHON OFFICIAL GUIDELINES

HACKSPECTRA NATIONAL HACKATHON

General Rules, Preliminary Round Format & Final Round Guidelines

Team Requirement Exactly 4 Members
Institution Same College / University
Preliminary Fee FREE (No Entry Fee)

GENERAL RULES

  • 01 A team must consist of exactly 4 members.
  • 02 All team members must belong to the same college/university.
  • 03 Participants must bring their own laptops, chargers, power adapters, extension boards, mouse, and any other accessories or equipment needed for development.
  • 04 Participants are expected to maintain professionalism, discipline, and decorum throughout the event and treat fellow participants, mentors, judges, and organizers with respect.
  • 05 Teams must adhere to the event schedule. Late submissions or delays may result in disqualification.
  • 06 Any form of plagiarism, cheating, or unfair means will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • 07 The decision of the judges and organizing committee shall be final and binding.
  • 08 Teams must create the repository using the organizer-provided GitHub account and must push their latest code at least once every hour throughout the hackathon to maintain a record of their progress.
  • 09 Sleeping beds will be provided by the organizers at night.

EVENT FORMAT

STAGE 01 PRELIMINARY ROUND

PPT PROPOSAL SUBMISSION

Teams must submit a PowerPoint presentation (PPT) presenting their proposed solution to a real-world problem. Teams are free to choose any domain, field, or area of interest and propose an innovative solution.

STAGE 02 FINAL ROUND (TOP 20 SHORTLISTED)

24H ON-SITE SPRINT
  • • The top 20 ideas from the preliminary round will be shortlisted for the finals.
  • • Shortlisted teams must build and present a working solution at the event venue.
  • • Finalists will be provided with an on-the-spot problem statement/theme during the event and will be required to design and build a working solution within the given time.
Note: Registration fees are applicable ONLY to teams shortlisted for the final round. No registration fee is required at the preliminary round stage.
